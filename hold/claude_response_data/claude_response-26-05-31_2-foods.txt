## Curator Notes

### FDC 2644286 & 2644292 — Structural Limitation

Both navy and pinto records belong to the **FNDDS 2021-2023** dataset (the same dataset as FDC 2644285/black, 2644287/cannellini, 2644294/great northern, all confirmed via myfooddata.com). This dataset was designed to support dietary recall analysis, not full nutrient profiling. Its records carry **only**: energy (kcal), total fat, protein, total carbohydrate, and the minerals Ca, Fe, K, Mg, P, Na, Zn, Cu, and Mn. Fiber, sugars, fat subfractions, all vitamins, and all amino acids are structurally absent from the source data.

**Recommendation for your DIAAS workflow:** For amino acid-based DIAAS calculations on navy and pinto beans, you will need to supplement with SR Legacy records. The best available USDA alternatives with full amino acid profiles are:

| Food | FDC ID | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Beans, navy, canned, drained solids | 173747 | SR Legacy |
| Beans, pinto, canned, drained solids | 174286 | SR Legacy |
| Beans, pinto, mature seeds, canned, drained solids, rinsed in tap water | 174289 | SR Legacy |

These SR Legacy records carry complete amino acid panels and are the highest-quality USDA source for beans in DIAAS-critical contexts.

### FDC 172465 — Computation Verification

The per-100g scaling from the 231g serving is exact. The starch value (8.66g/100g, scaled from 20g/231g) is present in the source but was omitted from the JSON because it is a carbohydrate subfraction rather than a top-level nutrient key in your schema. You may wish to add `starch_g` to your schema for refried beans if that matters for glycemic load calculations.
