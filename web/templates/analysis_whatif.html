Add a row for each change; they all apply together. Type part of a name in a food/recipe box and pick from the list (or type its code, such as U171477 or R21). Remove and Replace reach foods and recipes wherever they appear, including inside recipes. Add puts the amount on every day that has a meal logged. To swap one food for another only where the old one was eaten, use Replace; to drop one food and eat another every day, use a Remove row plus an Add row.
