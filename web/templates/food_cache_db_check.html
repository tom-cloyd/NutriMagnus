Missing nutrient data {{ manual_link("data-completeness") }}

Looks for cached foods with no values at all for a nutrient group (the same groups as a Nutritional Analysis table, plus the amino acids under Protein Quality). Macronutrients count as missing if any of calories, protein, carbohydrate, or fat is absent; amino acids only for a food that has protein. A group with some values is normal for real data and isn’t listed.

Not every gap matters — macronutrients for a spice, say, since too little is used to count. Check not needed for any gap like that and click Save “not needed” choices: NuMa stops listing it for that food, and leaves it out of any Claude AI prompt.

Food data problems {{ manual_link("calorie-checks") }}

Each food’s calories are checked against its own protein, carbohydrate and fat (4, 4 and 9 kcal per gram), and every food is checked for values that can’t be right: anything negative, more than 100 g of one nutrient per 100 g, more sugars than carbohydrate, or more of the three fat types than total fat. A food that arrived with protein, carbs and fat but no calories got an estimated figure automatically — listed here for information, not as a problem. A food whose macronutrients you’ve marked “not needed” above (a supplement, a spice) isn’t checked for calories.

Amounts that no longer match {{ manual_link("stale-amounts") }}

When you enter an amount as a volume or portion (“1/3 c”, “2 T”, “p1”), NuMa works out its grams once, then and there. If the food’s portions change later, or NuMa’s way of converting does, the stored grams stay as they were. These recipe ingredients and logged foods would come out different if entered today. Amounts entered with their own weight (“42 g”, “2 T 15 g”) are never listed. A volume with a weight in brackets after it (“1/3 c (42 gr)”) is: older versions of NuMa added that weight themselves, but it may be one you weighed, so check before updating (updating drops the bracketed figure).
