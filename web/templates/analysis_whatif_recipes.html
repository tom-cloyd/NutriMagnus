What-if: Recipes {{ manual_link("whatif-recipes") }}

See how your recipes' nutrition per serving would change if you removed, added, replaced or cut down an ingredient. Nothing is saved or changed — your recipes stay exactly as they are. For the effect on what you actually ate, use What-if: Meals.

Add a row for each change; they all apply together. Type part of a name in a food/recipe box and pick from the list. Remove, Replace and Change amount reach the ingredient wherever it is used, including inside sub-recipes.
