*UL = Tolerable Upper Intake Level: the highest average daily amount considered safe for nearly all healthy people. Built-in defaults come from NIH/IOM's DRI summary tables (12 nutrients, some age-banded); set your own personal max limit under Settings > Nutrient Targets to override a nutrient's default or add one not built in. {{ manual_link("maxlimits", "Learn more") }}

Special note re: UL scope. Niacin, folate, and magnesium each have an official UL, but it applies only to synthetic or fortified-food forms (supplements, fortified cereal, etc.) — not to how much of them occurs naturally in whole food. NuMa tracks your whole-food intake, so these three are deliberately left off the UL column here; you won't see a warning for them no matter how much ordinary food you log. If you take a supplement containing one of the three, set a custom max limit to watch that amount specifically. {{ manual_link("maxlimits", "Learn more") }}
