Ingredient comparison (per 100 g of each item)

Each ingredient's amount is rescaled to what it contributes to 100 g of the finished food or recipe, so recipes of different batch sizes — and a plain food alongside them — are all on the same footing. Ingredients used by more than one item are listed first and shaded grey. A food entry shows itself as its own single "ingredient" (100 g, by definition). A recipe missing a raw-ingredient weight for any component shows — throughout, since its 100 g basis can't be computed.
