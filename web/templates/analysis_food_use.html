Replace, remove or add a food or recipe in these meals {{ manual_link("fooduse-substitute") }}

Changes the meals selected above, all at once: replace one food or recipe with another (handy when a name change, or a food re-added under a new entry, has left what looks like two different foods, and you want to fold the older one into the newer), remove one from every meal it's in, or add one to every meal. Replace and remove reach only items added directly to a meal; a food used inside a recipe is part of that recipe's own ingredient list — change it from Food Use in Recipes instead. To see what a change would do to your nutrition before making it, use What-if.

How: choose what to do. To replace or remove, tick the box beside the food or recipe in the table below (once you've searched). To replace or add, start typing a name in the search box here and pick it from the list. It has to be in your Food Cache or Recipes already — to use a food you've never added, find it with Food Search first (opens in a new tab). If you already know the codes, you can type them straight into the code boxes instead. Only some days? Click pick dates beside a food's day count in the table and tick the dates you want: the change then reaches only the meals on those dates that the food appears in (for add, it's added alongside it in those meals).
