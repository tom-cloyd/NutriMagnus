Substitute a food or recipe {{ manual_link("fooduse-substitute") }}

Replace every ingredient occurrence of one food or recipe with another, across the recipes selected above — handy when a name change (or a food re-added under a new entry) has left what looks like two different foods, and you want to fold the older one into the newer. Every recipe changed has its DCP recomputed automatically, cascading up to any recipe that uses it as a sub-recipe in turn.

The table below (once you've searched) shows what's currently used in this selection — it isn't a list of foods you can replace with, and the item you're replacing appears in it too (marked "replacing this"). To find the ID of the food or recipe you want as the replacement, look it up first: Food Search (opens in a new tab — its results table shows each food's ID) or Recipes for a recipe's ID.
