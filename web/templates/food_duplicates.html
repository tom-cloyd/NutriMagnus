Duplicate Foods {{ manual_link("duplicate-foods") }}

Foods in your Food Cache with the same name, once capitals, punctuation, a “* ” starter prefix, “Copy of” and “(FDC …)” are ignored. Two copies of one food split your data: a fix to one doesn’t reach recipes and meals using the other.

For each group: compare the copies (Compare nutrition), then Keep the better one — usually the one with more nutrient values, or the one you’ve corrected. Every recipe, logged meal and pantry entry using the others is switched to it, and the others are deleted; each recipe or meal line keeps its own name and amount. If they really are different foods (one raw, one cooked, say), click Not duplicates and NuMa won’t list them together again.
